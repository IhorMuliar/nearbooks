# Public claims, checked

NEAR's economics are argued about in public, and the published numbers disagree with each other: an official dashboard, a treasury company's report, a research newsletter, analysts on X, the press. This page takes the most widely shared claims, as published, and grades each one against the same data the rest of this repository verifies.

The point is to explain the numbers, not to score points against their authors. Most claims hold once their dates and inputs are lined up; where one does not, the table says exactly what the chain shows instead.

- **Where the claims are kept:** `pipeline/claims.json`, with the source, the date, whether the text is a verbatim quote or our summary, and the source's own figures where the check needs them. They were read on 2026-10-21 and typed in by hand, like the published figures in `pipeline/config.json`.
- **How they are graded:** `pipeline/verify.py` grades each claim against `metrics.json`. Every verdict rests on a condition, and `make verify` fails if that condition stops holding.
- **What the dashboard shows:** the same table, read from `site/claims.json`, which `make verify` writes and checks.

## How the break-even figures line up

Three published "break-even" volumes differ by 6x: $177M a day (SVRN x The Tie, March), our $795M, and $1.13B (Latent Equity, October). They use the same arithmetic:

volume = new NEAR per day × NEAR price ÷ take rate

They differ in their inputs:
- **Price.** SVRN's figure was set at about $1.22 a NEAR and Latent Equity's at $5.90; ours uses $4.67.
- **Take rate.** Latent Equity uses about 4.6 bps; we measure 5.15 bps of captured revenue over the last 30 days.

Put on the same price, the three land within about 25% of each other.

All three describe a burn that is not happening. The NEAR bought back is held in a multisig, so under today's policy total supply grows at any volume. The figure is the volume at which buybacks would absorb new supply if the NEAR were burned. In NEAR it is simply about 87,600 NEAR of purchases a day, whatever the price.
